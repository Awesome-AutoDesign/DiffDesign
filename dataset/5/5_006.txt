Living room, dining room, open kitchen, guest room, pet room, public bathroom, and entryway. The kitchen can be located in the southeast or southwest corner. An open kitchen (with external access) with a bar can accommodate 5-6 people.
●Square dining tables are preferred over round ones. Curved sofas are preferred in the living room.
●The living room should be open-plan. Under the staircase, no storage should be provided; it should be a feature. Bay windows should remain intact.
●The original kitchen area should be converted into a pet room with a door and wet and dry areas for bathing.
