It was originally a classic three-bedroom apartment, but after looking at the dimensions, it seemed a little off in every aspect. Considering the scale, the sofa and dining table were planned on one side. The platform-style sofa and the dining booth add layers and give the space a casual feel. The balcony was divided into two, with one part incorporated into the living room. The booth is combined with the floor cabinet to weaken the boundary between the living room and the balcony. The other part is combined with the multi-functional room, leveraging each other to make the space transparent and the traffic lines flow back. No formal TV background wall is used, and the TV is used as a decoration as an additional function in the living room. This also reduces the cost.
