Both the master bedroom and the study have platforms, creating a nice height difference that creates a wonderful sense of space. Renovation is a process of compromise. Although it's a little inconvenient to walk around, it's truly wonderful and comfortable.
The open-plan study allows for simultaneous reading, figurines, and writing while watching TV. The open space connects the entire exterior, creating a wonderful space for communication and a homey atmosphere.
The design incorporates many echoing elements. I personally prefer to maintain a consistent design across the interior, with the exception of color. A closer look reveals a lot of design elements: the mirror and coffee table, the floor lamp and wall lamp, the wall lamp and cups, the bench and table and chairs. These elements all resonate throughout the home. This staggered arrangement creates a more cohesive whole.
