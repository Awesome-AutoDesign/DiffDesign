Hakka cultural elements seamlessly blend with modern space. The space's use of a time-honored ochre color scheme creates a sense of grandeur and stability while also bringing a touch of warmth. It's a haven for Shenzhen residents.

◼The large and small brick walls at the center of the shop juxtapose the Hakka walled houses, creating a sense of enclosure and integration. We interpret this with modern techniques, creating a sense of relative independence while harmoniously blending into the surroundings.

◼The aged red bricks and rattan chairs capture the traditional atmosphere. The numerous seemingly casual seating areas create a relaxing and cozy atmosphere unlike other milk tea and snack shops.
