The spatial planning draws inspiration from the Cui Linglong layout of Suzhou gardens, creating a three-fold circulation pattern within the store. This creates a rich visual experience as customers move through the store, with different areas of the space appearing one after another as they progress. This clever layout visually expands the limited space, creating a sense of continuous expansion, enriching the customer experience and adding a sense of exploration to the store.

The design incorporates Suzhou-style elements, striving to infuse the charm of Jiangnan into every detail: corridors, stone features, gardens, lanterns...

The refined brand identity creates a refined and gentle Jiangnan healing dessert shop.
