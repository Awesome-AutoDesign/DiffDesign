This project cleverly utilizes a 1.2-meter height difference from east to west, meticulously arranging the ground floor on multiple levels. This significantly enhances visual continuity and a sense of spatial hierarchy between activities.
The central open-air courtyard, the heart of the house, cleverly connects the horizontal and vertical spaces. Wooden pillars and flooring, with a modern touch, create a warm atmosphere, reminiscent of traditional Sri Lankan courtyards. Pure vertical lines, minimalist geometric forms, expansive open views, and a light-filled layout blur the boundaries between indoors and outdoors, creating a seamless sense of space.
White lacquered walls and terrazzo floors reflect natural light into every corner, creating a tranquil and spacious living atmosphere. The seamless design between the pantry and dining area further enhances the fluidity and symbiosis of the space. The kitchen is cleverly concealed next to the pantry, meeting functional requirements while maintaining a clean and aesthetically pleasing appearance. The overall design skillfully blends natural, historical, and modern elements to create a comfortable and charming living space.
