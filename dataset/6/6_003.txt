A few practical principles for floor planning in the new Chinese teahouse style.

Seating should be arranged based on a progression of privacy. You can refer to Starbucks' three-level seating arrangement for guidance.

70-75 cm bar stools: near entrances and exits, suitable for 1-2 people, for short stays.

43-45 cm chairs: located inwards, core seating: suitable for 2-6 people, for business meetings and gatherings.

35-40 cm low sofas: facing the view, suitable for 2-4 people, for gatherings and short breaks.

The core area should have a large bar counter and display area, creating a continuous circulation flow.

If possible, the interior should have two to three levels of staggered flooring to ensure an interesting space.
