With people at the center, our range of movement and visual field is confined to fan-shaped areas. Therefore, by fully integrating functional connections and behavioral scale through the lens of movement and perception, minimal space is required to meet basic needs. The remaining space can be "emptied out," creating more white space within the limited space, allowing for expansion and a closer connection to nature.

✦ Polyhedron

The polyhedron form breaks with conventional shapes and explores spatial relationships that interact with the environment. The "crooked" volume, inserted within the diagonal layout, perfectly accommodates the growth of the space, in effect freeing up space.

✦ Shared Form

Interactive visual perspectives and functional layouts create an open and integrated space, fostering a free-flowing living experience. A V-shaped work area for two, bridging the gap between open work and shared forms, achieves a micro-organizational layout and free-flowing activities.
