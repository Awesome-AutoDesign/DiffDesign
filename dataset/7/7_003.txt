The space radiates outward from the rotunda. There's no clear boundary between the craft brewing space and the gallery; the spatial changes are only indicated by the height difference. This allows for significant flexibility in exhibition layout, allowing installations and interactive exhibits to be cleverly integrated into the craft brewing space. The two spaces connect and intertwine, creating a sense of wandering between the craft brewing space and the gallery.
[Four R's] The rotunda's interior utilizes existing structural beams to form a central corridor, which, combined with multiple openings, creates a multi-viewing space.
[Five R's] The circulation space is not only a vertical element connecting different levels, but also a functional "artwork." Steps extend from the bar area to the exhibition area, serving not only as a connecting staircase but also as booths or seating for events. The combination of the steps and the wall creates a rich visual connection space.
