The Haworth/HWS Experience Center highlights the positive interplay between nature and technology, furnishings and space, business etiquette and humanistic care. Each showroom has its own distinct style. In its pursuit of differentiation, the Haworth/HWS Experience Center evokes a sense of "urban backyard," offering a sense of intimacy, nature, and warmth. At the same time, it strikes a balance between these elements within the limited space.

3⃣️The Gensler design team created a "third space" for employees, offering a distinct experience from the office area and fostering the HWS team atmosphere of collaboration, health, progress, and joy. Spaces such as an open bar, yoga area, reading corner, and nursing room allow employees to relax and connect in a relaxed and comfortable manner. (The "third space" is designed to enhance efficiency, health, and social interaction, fostering collaboration while providing a space for employees to recharge and rejuvenate.)
