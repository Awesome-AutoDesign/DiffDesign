The gallery is located on the ground floor of the mall, facing the atrium on one side and along the mall's inner street on the other. The original space was narrow and monotonous, but the all-glass exterior makes it feel like part of the passageway itself. To guide visitors inside, Zhizuo Studio introduced a series of white walls with arches. These walls fold and extend along the shorter sides of the site, creating separate areas facing inward and outward. From the street level, one can see the interior space opening and closing in a constant state of flux. This folding wall seems to extend the corridor into the gallery, inviting visitors to explore the store.

[Medium Skin Tone R] The simple white arched walls not only create a rhythmic facade but also create a distinct layering of the interior space. They also cleverly provide ample display surface and flexible space for the gallery.
