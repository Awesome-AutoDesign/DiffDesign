The design concept for RIPPLE draws inspiration from the site's natural landscape. Facing Yuehai Lake, the project features cascading ripples as its core design concept. Ripples can be enclosed or gradually increasing, and these concepts are emphasized in the enclosed volumes and distinctive materials used within the interior.

Creating connections was a key design consideration. The coffee bar welcomes guests upon entering through the main entrance. With the curved glass doors fully opened, the outdoor space becomes an extension of the café, seamlessly connecting indoor and outdoor areas.

The bar's unique shape provides an opportunity for guests to chat and connect with the baristas. A long communal table, strategically located in the center of the space, allows for community dining experiences and offers a view of the entire coffee-making process. Views from all interior dining areas encourage guests to linger while enjoying the views.
