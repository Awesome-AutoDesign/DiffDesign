The area is divided into private and public areas. Private areas are defined by separate rooms with specific functions. Public areas represent the spaces in between, including lobbies, meeting rooms, and exhibition areas. These areas encompass a variety of streets, squares, and alleys, divided by walls in a variety of straight and curved lines. This reorganizes the site into an urban character.
To describe the space, urban design terms were employed, informed by human behavior: this is both a city and a garden. The gap between old and new is the backyard of an office. Green is the characteristic of the straight walls, while the illuminated ceiling is as bright as a courtyard.
